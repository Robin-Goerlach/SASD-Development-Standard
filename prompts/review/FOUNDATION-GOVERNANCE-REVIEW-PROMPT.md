# Foundation and Governance Review Prompt

Review the SASD Development Standard Foundation and Governance documents as a cautious maintainer.

Check:

1. vision, scope and target groups,
2. conflicts between principles and normative rules,
3. document ownership and dependencies,
4. normative language and requirement testability,
5. lifecycle and approval transitions,
6. versioning and change compatibility,
7. exceptions and Alignment semantics,
8. practicality for solo developers and small teams,
9. security, privacy and legal overclaims,
10. release readiness.

Classify findings as Blocker, Major, Minor or Observation. Do not mark a document Approved merely because it is complete or machine-valid. Separate review readiness, approval and publication.
