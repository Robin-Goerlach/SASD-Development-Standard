# Standard Change Review Checklist

- [ ] Change type is classified.
- [ ] Problem and intended outcome are clear.
- [ ] Affected document and requirement IDs are listed.
- [ ] Compatibility and migration impact are assessed.
- [ ] Quality levels and profiles are reviewed.
- [ ] Security, privacy, legal and operational effects are reviewed.
- [ ] Templates, checklists, prompts and tooling are reviewed.
- [ ] New obligations have a proportional benefit and evidence model.
- [ ] Requirement IDs are not reused.
- [ ] Generated indexes are refreshed.
- [ ] Validators pass.
- [ ] Changelog and target version are updated.
- [ ] Decision and rationale are recorded.
